When money runs short, you need a person to talk to, not a maze of websites. The [Folsom Cordova Community Partnership](https://www.thefccp.org/community-support) (FCCP) has been that person for families in Rancho Cordova and Folsom for more than 30 years. Its Community Support program offers emergency supplies, referrals and help connecting with SMUD's EnergyHELP bill assistance.

The FCCP began in 1992 as a schools foundation. Folsom and Rancho Cordova share a school board, and the organization grew out of that shared community. Today it serves families and youth in Rancho Cordova, Folsom and nearby areas of eastern Sacramento County.

## How Community Support Works in Rancho Cordova

Community Support started in 2008. The FCCP describes it as resources, referrals, short-term case management and emergency basic-needs supplies. It's often the first door a family walks through, and staff can point you toward other help from there.

Safety Net services run during set hours each week. The FCCP's website doesn't publish those hours, so call before you go.

Depending on your situation, staff can connect you with partners for:

- Food
- Clothing
- Childcare
- Employment
- Housing help
- Other local resources

## SMUD EnergyHELP and the FCCP

The FCCP is one of six charity partners that handle SMUD's EnergyHELP program. EnergyHELP uses donations to pay electric bills for customers at risk of losing power.

SMUD sets the rules, so it helps to know them:

- You must be at risk of having your power shut off in the next few days.
- You must be enrolled in SMUD's Energy Assistance Program Rate.
- You must need up to $200 to keep your power on.
- Each person can receive up to $200 in a 12-month period.

SMUD builds a randomized daily list of qualifying customers and contacts some of them, depending on available funds. Help is not guaranteed. If your bill is overdue, call SMUD at 1-888-742-7683 as well, and ask the FCCP about other support.

## How to Reach the FCCP

1. Call [916-361-8684](tel:+19163618684) or email [CS@theFCCP.org](mailto:CS@theFCCP.org).
2. Tell staff what you're facing, whether it's food, a bill or something else.
3. Visit the Family Resource Center at 10665 Coloma Road, Suite 200, Rancho Cordova, CA 95670.

There's more under one roof, which is helpful when one problem leads to several others. The FCCP also runs parent education and home visiting, youth programs, and employment services. It operates one of twelve America's Job Centers in Sacramento County, and it also provides employment services for refugee and asylee job seekers.

You don't have to figure this out alone. Asking about help with a bill or a bag of groceries is not a failure, and the people at the Family Resource Center hear from neighbors in the same spot every week. A phone call is a fine place to start, and staff can tell you which of their services fits what you're facing right now.
