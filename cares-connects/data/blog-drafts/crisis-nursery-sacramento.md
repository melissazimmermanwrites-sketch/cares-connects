**If you or someone in your home is in immediate danger or thinking of suicide, call or text [988](tel:988) or call 911. If you are a parent who needs a safe place for your child right now, call the Crisis Nursery.**

Some days a family runs out of options all at once. A hospital stay. A night with no place to sleep. A mental health crisis. A job emergency with no one to watch the kids. The [Sacramento Children's Home Crisis Nursery](https://www.kidshome.org/what-we-do/crisis-nursery-program/) is built for exactly that moment. It offers free emergency child care for children from birth through age five, for families across Sacramento County.

The program is voluntary. You choose to bring your child, and the service costs nothing. Asking for temporary care is a responsible decision, and it is exactly what the program was designed to support. Partial funding comes from the First 5 Sacramento Commission, through Proposition 10 tobacco tax revenue, along with individual donors.

## What the Crisis Nursery Offers Families in Sacramento

Parents bring their children when circumstances feel overwhelming, and the staff understand that the reasons vary from one family to the next. The nursery lists illness, domestic violence, homelessness and treatment needs as examples. You don't have to wait until things fall apart completely. Asking for help early is the point.

Care is available 24 hours a day, every day of the year. A child can stay for a few hours or overnight. The longest stay is up to 30 days.

## Two Sacramento Locations

There are two nurseries, and each has its own phone line. Call the one closest to you.

- **North Crisis Nursery:** 4533 Pasadena Avenue, Sacramento, CA 95821. Phone: [916-679-3600](tel:+19166793600)
- **South Crisis Nursery:** 6699 South Land Park Drive, Sacramento, CA 95831. Phone: [916-394-2000](tel:+19163942000)

The Children's Home's main number is [916-452-3981](tel:+19164523981).

## How to Use the Crisis Nursery

Getting started is simple:

1. Call the North or South nursery and explain what's happening.
2. Talk with the staff about what your child needs and what your family is facing.
3. Bring your child to the nursery you've arranged to visit.

Spaces can be limited, so call before you head over. You don't need a referral. You don't need to be involved with any agency.

## Why This Matters

Stress piles up fast when you're parenting alone, or when every adult in the house is stretched thin. Having a safe place for your little one can give you room to deal with the emergency in front of you, whether that means getting to a hospital, finding somewhere to stay tonight, or simply getting through a crisis without worrying about who is watching your child. It also helps keep a hard week from turning into something worse, and that is the whole idea behind the program.

The Crisis Nursery is a place to ask for help without judgment. You know your child best. Sometimes you just need someone to hold the other end of the rope.

### FAQ

**Who can use the Crisis Nursery?**
Parents and caregivers in Sacramento County with children from birth through age five.

**Does it cost anything?**
No. The service is free.

**Is it open at night?**
Yes. The nursery describes its emergency child care as available 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
