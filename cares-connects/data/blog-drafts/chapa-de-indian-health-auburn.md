[Chapa-De Indian Health](https://chapa-de.org/) runs the Auburn Health Center on Atwood Road in Auburn, Placer County. It offers medical, dental, vision and behavioral health care. Its first priority is American Indians, Alaska Natives and low-income neighbors.

Chapa-De began in 1974 with a single dental chair in rented office space in Auburn. It grew out of a community survey on the health of American Indians in Placer, Sierra Nevada and El Dorado counties. The name comes from a Nisenan term that loosely means where the valley meets the trees or foothills. From that one chair, the program has grown over five decades into a full health center, and it still keeps its focus on the people it was built to serve. Today it operates two community health centers and reports about 25,000 active patients.

Chapa-De's supporting Tribe is the United Auburn Indian Community, and members of that Tribe sit on its board.

## Who Chapa-De Serves in Auburn

The website says the main priority is American Indians, Alaska Natives and low-income patients. It also says care is provided regardless of financial situation or ethnicity. So you don't have to be Native to be a patient.

Here's how cost works:

- **Verified American Indians and Alaska Natives:** Under its contract with the Indian Health Service, Chapa-De provides no-cost or low-cost services and medications.
- **Everyone else:** Chapa-De accepts Medi-Cal and Medicare. Uninsured patients can use a sliding fee scale based on income.

## Services at the Auburn Health Center

The Auburn clinic lists these services:

- Primary medical care
- Dental care
- Optometry
- Behavioral health
- Pharmacy
- Prenatal care
- Substance use disorder care
- Telehealth
- Health and wellness groups and classes

## How to Become a Patient

The website doesn't list new patient steps, so the best move is a phone call.

1. Call the Auburn clinic at [530-887-2800](tel:+15308872800).
2. Ask about becoming a new patient.
3. If you're American Indian or Alaska Native, ask what documents you'll need to show.

The center is at 11670 Atwood Road, Auburn, CA 95603. It's open 8 am to 5 pm, closed 12 to 1 pm. Current patients can schedule through the Patient Portal or the healow app.

## A Note on Current Services

Chapa-De has posted a notice about water damage in part of the Auburn medical building. It affects medical pods, behavioral health and substance use disorder services, and in-person appointments may be limited. The pharmacy isn't affected. Call ahead and check the website's incident update page for the latest.

Because it's a nonprofit community health center, Chapa-De gathers most of what a family needs under one roof. A parent can pick up a prescription at the pharmacy after a dental visit, and a teenager can see a counselor in the same building where a grandparent sees a primary care provider.

## What's Coming

Chapa-De says a new clinic in Rocklin is under construction, with an aim of opening in early 2027. That news is good for South Placer families who now drive to Auburn.

Good care close to home matters, and a clinic that has been here for 50 years knows its neighbors.
