**If you are a veteran in crisis, dial [988](tel:988) then press 1 to reach the Veterans Crisis Line. Help is there any time.**

[Nation's Finest](https://nationsfinest.org/) supports veterans and their families with housing, health care connections and employment help. You may know it by an older name, Veterans Resource Centers of America. The Sacramento office sits at 7270 East Southgate Drive and serves veterans in Sacramento, Yolo, Placer and Nevada counties.

The organization says it has been serving veterans and their families for nearly 50 years. Across California, Arizona and Nevada it has 30 locations in 15 mostly rural communities and serves more than 6,800 veterans a year. Its staff are mostly veterans or members of veteran families themselves.

## Who Nation's Finest Serves

The organization says it serves all veterans, regardless of branch of service or status of discharge. That matters. Many people assume a less-than-honorable discharge closes every door. Call and ask before you decide it does.

## Services for Veterans in Sacramento

Every program starts with a conversation. Veterans meet a case manager first, who does an initial assessment and builds a personal service plan. The plan covers things like housing, mental health, medical needs, money, school and work.

Services across Nation's Finest include:

- Transitional housing for homeless veterans
- Permanent supportive housing
- Case management
- Mental health services
- Employment services
- Homelessness prevention
- Mobile service units

The organization has more than 300 units of permanent supportive housing for veterans and their families. One of them is Mather Veterans Village in Rancho Cordova, which opened in 2016 and was built with Mercy Housing California. Residents there have access to case management, health and wellness resources, benefits navigation and employment help.

## How to Get Help From Nation's Finest in Sacramento

1. Call the Sacramento office at [916-393-8387](tel:+19163938387). It's open Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm.
2. Or call the statewide line at [1-833-468-9676](tel:+18334689676) (1-833-GOT-YOR6) and ask to be connected.
3. Meet with a case manager for an assessment and a plan.

The Sacramento office is at 7270 East Southgate Drive, Sacramento, CA 95823. If you're not sure what you need, say so. The organization invites you to call and be connected with the right help.

## A Note on Crisis Care

Nation's Finest is a service provider, not an emergency line. If you or a veteran you love is in danger, use the Veterans Crisis Line first: dial 988 then press 1.

Veterans have already given a lot. Asking for help is not a weakness. It's one more mission, and you don't have to do it alone. Whether you need a roof, a job lead, someone to sort through your benefits, or just a straight answer about where to start, a call to the Southgate Drive office is a practical first step, and the staff there understand the road you're on.
