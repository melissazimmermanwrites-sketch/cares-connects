[The HUB](https://www.wpusd.org/thehub) is the family resource center of Western Placer Unified School District in Lincoln, California. It connects students and families with services for "whole-child development and family well-being." If a school day is hard because something at home is hard, this is a good place to start.

You don't have to be a parent in crisis to walk in. The HUB is built for families who need a little extra to get through a school year.

## What The HUB Offers Lincoln Families

The HUB's website lists these services:

- Shelf-stable food
- Produce and local food distributions
- Backpacks and school supplies
- Hygiene kits
- College and career support
- Referrals to county and local services

The site also links to a separate Work Permits page for students. Details on that page are best confirmed with the district.

## Where The HUB Is in Lincoln

The HUB is at 790 J Street, Lincoln, CA 95648. It's in the annex buildings on the north side of the main parking lot at Lincoln High School, between the Lincoln High and Phoenix High campuses.

Phone: [(916) 434-3760](tel:+19164343760)

For district-level questions, the district office is at 600 Sixth Street, Suite 400, Lincoln, CA 95648, phone [(916) 645-6350](tel:+19166456350).

Many families in western Placer County are balancing rent, work schedules and school paperwork at the same time. The HUB gives them one local address to call for practical help, so they don't have to search through several websites to find a food distribution, a backpack or a referral to counseling.

## Events at The HUB

The HUB runs events through the school year. As of this writing, the website lists these for November and December 2026:

- November 9, 10 am to 2 pm: Medi-Cal and CalFresh support, with help in Spanish
- November 18, 8 am to 1 pm: Donation intake
- November 18, 11 am to 1 pm: Food distribution
- December 2, 8 am to 1 pm: Donation intake

Dates change, so check the website before you go.

## How to Get Help from The HUB

The website doesn't spell out eligibility or regular open hours, and it doesn't describe a sign-up process. Here's what to do:

1. Call [(916) 434-3760](tel:+19164343760) and say what your family needs.
2. Ask when food distributions or other events are happening.
3. Ask about referrals if you need housing, counseling or youth services. The HUB points families to county and local programs.

You can also stop by the J Street address. Because the hours aren't posted, calling first will save you a trip.

## How to Support The HUB

Donation intake days are listed on the events calendar. If you want to give, check the site for what the HUB is accepting before you drop anything off.

Schools work best when families feel supported, and a student can't focus on homework if the pantry at home is empty. The HUB puts that idea to work in Lincoln, right next to the high school, so help is close to where students already spend their days. If you're a neighbor or a local business, a call to the HUB is also the best way to ask how you can pitch in.
