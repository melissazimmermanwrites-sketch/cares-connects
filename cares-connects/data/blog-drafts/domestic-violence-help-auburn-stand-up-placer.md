**If you need help now: call the Stand Up Placer 24/7 crisis line at [1-800-575-5352](tel:+18005755352). You can also reach the National Domestic Violence Hotline at [1-800-799-7233](tel:+18007997233). If you are in immediate danger, call 911.**

If you're reading this because you're afraid, or because you're worried about someone else, you've already taken an important first step. [Stand Up Placer](https://standupplacer.org/) is Placer County's agency for survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault and sex trafficking. It serves people in Auburn, Roseville, Rocklin, Lincoln and the rest of the county.

## About Stand Up Placer

Stand Up Placer was formerly PEACE for Families. In 2012 the agency took its current name to show Placer County that it was time to take a stand against violence, and its mission today is to save lives by empowering survivors and educating communities.

The agency reports 33,194 safe bed nights provided in 2024.

## What Stand Up Placer Offers Survivors in Placer County

Services listed on the website include:

- A 24/7 crisis helpline
- 24/7 webchat through the website
- A confidential support request form
- Emergency safe housing
- Domestic Violence Emergency Response Team (DVERT) support
- Individual and group therapy
- Legal assistance
- Programs for sexual assault and sex trafficking survivors

Children are included as well, and the agency has offered an eight-week therapy group for children ages 5 to 10. Children need to be eligible for Stand Up Placer services to join, and you can check by calling (916) 773-7273.

## The Safe House

Stand Up Placer runs a confidential emergency shelter. It serves survivors who are leaving, or trying to leave, life-threatening violence, along with their minor and adult dependents. It welcomes all genders and accepts pets.

The location is kept private to protect everyone inside, so you can't simply walk up to the door, and access instead goes through the crisis line, where an advocate talks with you about your safety and what you need.

## How to Get Help

1. Call [1-800-575-5352](tel:+18005755352) any time, day or night.
2. Or use the webchat or the Confidential Support Request form on the [Get Help Now page](https://standupplacer.org/get-help-now/).
3. Tell the advocate what's happening, and remember that you decide how much to share.
4. To ask about eligibility for services, call [(916) 773-7273](tel:+19167737273).

The website offers a quick-exit option if you need to leave the page quickly, and if someone might be monitoring your phone or computer, consider using a different device that they cannot access.

Leaving is hard, and staying can feel just as hard, but you don't need a plan to reach out. You just need to make the call.

### FAQ

**Do I have to be ready to leave to call?** No. Advocates can talk with you about safety whatever stage you're at, and you can call just to ask questions, learn what's available, or work out what staying safer might look like for you and your children.

**Can I get help if I'm not sure it counts as abuse?** Yes. If something about your relationship frightens you or makes you feel unsafe, that is reason enough to call the crisis line and talk it through with an advocate who will listen.
