[The Salt Mine](https://www.thesaltmine.org/) is a faith-based nonprofit in Lincoln, California, that gives out food to neighbors in Lincoln and Sheridan. Its Food Closet hands out fresh and pantry boxes, and its thrift store helps pay for the work.

The Salt Mine was founded in 1985. It's a 501(c)(3) public charity, legally Vine Life Ministries, Inc., and it's funded by private donations and thrift store sales. It grew out of The Salt Mine Church, a non-denominational Christian church that started the Food Closet, and the website doesn't ask you to belong to any church before you receive food.

## The Salt Mine Food Closet in Lincoln, Placer County

The numbers show how many neighbors rely on this place, and the families behind them are often juggling rent, work and school schedules. In 2025, The Salt Mine reports it served more than 1,700 families. About 75 percent of those families included children, and about 30 percent included seniors.

The Food Closet serves residents of Lincoln and Sheridan only, and staff verify where you live. If you live somewhere else in Placer County, the website points you to a county resource list instead.

## What You Can Get at the Food Closet

The Salt Mine offers two kinds of boxes:

- **Fresh Food Box:** Available every open day. It holds produce, dairy, deli items, bread and pastry. Contents vary.
- **Pantry Box:** Available once a month, on weekdays. It holds non-perishable items and frozen meat. Contents vary.

For neighbors without housing, there's more:

- Daypacks with food you can eat on the go or cook without utensils
- Laundry and a clothing exchange, Tuesday through Friday
- Showers, Tuesday through Friday, for a limited time each day

Holiday turkeys and food boxes start in early November. If you already have a verified account, you're signed up automatically. If not, you can sign up during open hours with proof that you live in Lincoln or Sheridan.

## How to Get Help from The Salt Mine

The Food Closet works as curbside pickup, and the website doesn't mention an appointment, so you can simply arrive during open hours. Here's how it works:

1. Come during open hours: Tuesday through Friday, 9 am to 12 pm, or Saturday, 12 to 1 pm. It's closed Sunday and Monday.
2. Drive up on the 6th Street side. Staff will help you there. If a line forms, leave room for through traffic.
3. Bring something that verifies your identity and your address. Household members need to be verified too.

The address is 590 Lincoln Blvd., Lincoln, CA 95648. You can call [916-645-3778](tel:+19166453778) with questions before you go. The website has details on verifying household members.

## How to Help

Food donations are accepted Tuesday through Saturday, 9 am to 12 pm, and the site asks for food items only. Shopping or donating at the thrift store also keeps the Food Closet stocked.

The Salt Mine works alongside other Lincoln groups, including St. Vincent de Paul, to connect neighbors with help. Asking for food isn't something to feel bad about, because a box of groceries handed across a curb is just one neighbor helping another through a hard stretch.
