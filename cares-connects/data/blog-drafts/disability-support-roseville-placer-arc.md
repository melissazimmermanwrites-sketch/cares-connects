If you love someone with an intellectual or developmental disability, you know the question that comes after school ends: what happens next? [The Arc of Placer County](https://www.placerarc.org/), known locally as Placer ARC, is one answer. It runs adult day programs in Roseville and Auburn for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

## A Placer County Story Since 1958

Placer ARC began with parents. In 1958, families in Placer County started advocating for their children's right to an education. The group became a 501(c)(3) nonprofit by 1963 and opened its first sheltered workshop in an Auburn church basement in 1964.

Over the years the organization has been known as Advocacy, Resources & Choices and then Placer ARC. Today it's a chapter of The Arc of the United States. It describes itself as a private nonprofit with a volunteer board, supported by individuals in the community. Its adult day programs serve around 350 clients each day.

## Programs in Roseville and Auburn

Placer ARC lists three programs on its website:

- **Roseville Adult Center:** An adult day program at 531 Vernon Street in Roseville.
- **Adult Achievement Center:** An adult day program at 3217 Professional Drive in Auburn.
- **Studio 700 Center for the Arts:** The organization's arts program.

The organization also mentions a social recreation program for adults and a youth program for ages 16 and up. The website gives few details on what a day looks like at each program, so it's worth calling to ask what fits your family member's goals.

## Who Placer ARC Serves

Placer ARC serves people with intellectual and developmental disabilities in Placer County and the surrounding area. Its About page also mentions people diagnosed with a developmental disability or at risk of one. The website doesn't list specific admission criteria.

## How to Ask About Services

The website doesn't spell out the enrollment steps, so the simplest path is a phone call.

1. Call the Roseville Adult Center at [(916) 783-5700](tel:+19167835700), or the Adult Achievement Center in Auburn at [(530) 537-2647](tel:+15305372647).
2. You can also call the Executive Office at [(916) 781-3016](tel:+19167813016). It's located at 9273 Sierra College Boulevard, Roseville.
3. Ask about enrollment, funding and whether there's a place for your family member.

Many families also work with a service coordinator at their regional center. It's reasonable to ask that person about Placer ARC too.

Every adult deserves a place to learn, work, make art and be known by name. Placer ARC has spent more than 60 years building that place in Placer County.

### FAQ

**Is Placer ARC only in Roseville?** No. The organization runs its Adult Achievement Center in Auburn as well as the Roseville Adult Center, and each center has its own phone number, so you can call whichever one is closer to home and ask your questions there.

**Do I need a diagnosis to ask about services?** The website says Placer ARC serves people with intellectual and developmental disabilities, and it mentions people at risk of a developmental disability, so the best way to find out where your family member fits is simply to call and describe the situation.
