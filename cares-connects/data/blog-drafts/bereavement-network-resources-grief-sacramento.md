When someone you love dies, you may not know where to turn. [Bereavement Network Resources of Sacramento (BNRS)](https://griefhelpsacramento.com/) helps you find the right grief support in Sacramento and nearby counties. It points you to local groups, so you don't have to search alone.

BNRS is a volunteer-governed 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Its 2026 directory says it has served the Sacramento area since 1984. It was started by volunteers who had been bereaved themselves and saw a need to help others.

## What BNRS Does in Sacramento

BNRS is a referral and resource group, not a counseling provider. Think of it as the front door. It helps you find the group or service that fits your loss.

Its main tool is the Community Bereavement Resource Directory for Sacramento and surrounding counties, which is updated every two years. The 2026 edition is posted at [griefhelpsacramento.com/directory](https://griefhelpsacramento.com/directory/). BNRS also holds workshops and conferences and runs a bereavement speakers bureau.

## Types of Grief Support Listed

The website's calendar lists groups for different losses. Examples include:

- General grief support, including free "Living With Loss" sessions at Mercy General Hospital
- Support for people who are widowed
- "Hear to Heal" for loss from opioids or fentanyl
- Pet loss support through the Sacramento SPCA
- A Grief Wise Circle for men and male-identifying teens

Meeting days and times change, so check the calendar or call before you go. Many groups are free, but each group sets its own terms.

Some groups meet in person and some meet on Zoom. The widowed group, for example, meets Sunday afternoons in person, while the pet loss group meets online on the first Tuesday of each month. Each listing is run by a different host, so details like location and registration come from that host.

## How to Get Help from BNRS

You can start in a few ways:

1. Call [916-557-5882](tel:+19165575882).
2. Open the online directory and look for your type of loss.
3. Use the contact form on the website.

BNRS's mailing address is P.O. Box 660365, Sacramento, CA 95866. It's a mailing address only, not an office to visit.

## A Word About Grief

There's no right timeline. You might feel numb, angry or tired. You might feel fine, then not fine. If you aren't ready to sit in a room with strangers, you can start by simply reading through the directory at home. A room of people who've been there can make a hard season feel a little less lonely.

BNRS doesn't list crisis lines of its own. If your grief feels unsafe, use the numbers at the top of this page. Groups are ongoing support, not emergency help.

If you're a counselor, chaplain or volunteer who works with grieving people, BNRS also invites professionals to its networking and education events.
