[Roberts Family Development Center](https://robertsfdc.org/) is a nonprofit based at 770 Darina Avenue in North Sacramento, California. It runs after-school programs, mentoring and summer learning for K-12 students in North Sacramento, which includes Del Paso Heights, and across the greater Sacramento area. It also works with parents.

The center began in 2001 with 13 students. Derrell Roberts and his wife, Tina, started it in North Sacramento. Today the website says it serves more than 500 students and their families, mostly at seven sites in neighborhoods facing high risk.

## Youth Programs in Del Paso Heights, Sacramento County

The center describes its approach as serving each member of a family. That means academic support for kids, education for parents and a voice in the community. Here's what the website lists for young people:

- **After School Program:** Academic support, tutoring and enrichment for low-income students. It's built for families who struggle to find quality care and academic support for their children after the school day ends.
- **Mentoring:** One-on-one or small group support that helps students gain new skills, build social and emotional strength, and form positive relationships. The mentoring program is called Food for Thought.
- **Freedom Schools:** Listed among the center's programs. Ask about dates, ages and sites.
- **Pacers Moving Forward:** A college prep initiative.

The website doesn't list grade levels for each program, program costs or daily hours. Ask when you call, and don't assume a fee is a barrier before you've asked, because the answer may surprise you.

## Parents and the Neighborhood

The center also offers parent education and empowerment, and community advocacy. The site says it serves as the North Sacramento and Del Paso Heights lead for the county's initiative to reduce African American child deaths. It also leads a coalition of more than 30 North Sacramento and Del Paso Heights agencies.

Services are open to individuals and families of all races, ethnicities and communities, and the center asks families to join in as partners in their children's learning.

## How to Enroll or Ask a Question

1. Call [916-646-6631](tel:+19166466631) during office hours, Monday through Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.
2. Or email info@robertsfdc.org.
3. Tell staff your child's age and school, and ask which site and program fit best.

The center's address is listed as 770 Darina Avenue, Sacramento, CA 95815. One page on the website shows 766 Darina Avenue, so confirm before you drive over.

## What Families Can Expect

You want your child to feel safe, to catch up in a subject that feels hard and to have an adult who knows their name. The center's programs are built around those wishes. If your child is in elementary, middle or high school and could use some steady help, a phone call is all it takes to begin, and you can ask every question you have.
