**In immediate danger? Call 911. For 24/7 phone support, call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.**

The [Mental Health Urgent Care Clinic](https://www.tpcp.org/program/urgent-care/) in Sacramento is a walk-in clinic for people in a mental health crisis. It serves Sacramento County residents of all ages, and it's run by the nonprofit Turning Point Community Programs. Think of it as an alternative to the emergency room when what you need is a calm, trained person to talk with.

This is a clinic, not a phone line. You go in person. If you need to talk right now from home, 988 is the number to use, and the clinic is there when you're ready to come in.

## Who the Sacramento Urgent Care Clinic Serves

The clinic helps people in Sacramento County who are in a mental health crisis, a substance use crisis, or both. Services are voluntary. You don't need a referral, and you don't need an appointment. The clinic says it serves people regardless of their ability to pay.

The clinic is funded by Sacramento County's Division of Behavioral Health Services. It holds CARF accreditation, which is an independent review of the quality of its care.

## What Happens at the Clinic

You walk in, and the team meets you where you are. Based on your situation, the clinic offers:

- A crisis assessment that looks at mental health and substance use together
- Crisis intervention and problem-solving, with the aim of avoiding a hospital stay
- A psychiatric medication evaluation (the clinic doesn't prescribe controlled medications)
- Peer and family support
- Referrals and links to ongoing care and community supports

The goal is to help you get through the crisis safely, and to leave with a plan and a few people or programs you can turn to next, so the support doesn't end when you walk out the door.

## Hours and Address in Sacramento

The clinic is at 2130 Stockton Blvd., Building 300, Sacramento, CA 95817.

- Monday through Friday: 8 am to midnight. The last walk-in is at 10 pm.
- Saturday, Sunday and holidays: 8 am to 8 pm. The last walk-in is at 6 pm.

Questions before you go? Call [916-520-2460](tel:+19165202460).

## How to Get Help

1. Come to the clinic during open hours. Plan to arrive before the last walk-in time.
2. Check in at the front desk. No appointment or referral is needed.
3. Meet with the team for an assessment and a plan for what comes next.

If you're caring for someone else, you can go with them. The clinic offers family support too.

## A Place to Land

Not every hard moment is an emergency that needs a 911 call, and not every hard moment can wait for a regular appointment weeks from now. That in-between space is where this clinic fits. A crisis can make every option feel like the wrong one. A walk-in clinic with trained staff, open late and on weekends, gives you another choice. You don't have to wait for things to get worse, and you don't have to sit in an emergency room waiting room to be heard.
