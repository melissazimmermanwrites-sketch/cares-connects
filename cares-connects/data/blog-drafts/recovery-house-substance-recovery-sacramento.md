Deciding to get help is hard, and finding a safe place to do it can be harder. [Sacramento Recovery House](https://www.sacrecovery.org/) in Sacramento, California, offers structured, live-in recovery programs for adults: one for men, and one for women called Gateway House for Women.

## About Sacramento Recovery House

The men's program started in 1964. Gateway House for Women started in 1968. The two organizations merged in 2013. Today both programs describe their care as gender responsive and trauma informed, and both list licenses from the California Department of Health Care Services. Staff are described as certified, and counselors follow California's alcohol and drug counselor regulations.

The men's house has 17 beds and uses a social model. That means recovery happens in a home setting, with peers, rather than in a clinic.

## What the Programs Look Like in Sacramento

Both programs offer 30, 60 or 90 day stays. Residents take part in:

- Daily classes on substance use
- Individual and group meetings
- At least one community 12-Step meeting each day
- Scheduled household duties and chores
- Time with clean and sober members of the community

Gateway House also offers counseling and education for residents and the people close to them.

## Who Can Apply

Both programs serve adults 18 and older. According to the admission policy, you should:

- Be free of drugs and alcohol for at least 72 hours
- Be free of alcoholic or epileptic seizures
- Have a current TB test
- Be willing to take part fully in the program
- Complete an intake interview, including a full medical history

The program is non-medical, and no regular health care is available on site. If you need medical detox first, ask about that when you call, so you know what to expect before you arrive.

## How to Get Started

1. Call the men's program at [(916) 455-6258](tel:+19164556258) or Gateway House for Women at [(916) 451-9312](tel:+19164519312).
2. Ask about openings and the intake interview.
3. Ask about cost and how it is paid. The website doesn't list fees, so it's best to ask directly.

The organization's website lists 1914 22nd Street, Sacramento, CA 95816. Confirm the address of the program you're calling about before you travel.

Recovery rarely happens alone. A bed, a daily routine and peers who understand what you're facing can be a steady place to start, and a phone call is all it takes to ask your first questions.
