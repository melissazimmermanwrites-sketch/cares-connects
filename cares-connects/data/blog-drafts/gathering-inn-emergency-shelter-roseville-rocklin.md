[The Gathering Inn](https://www.thegatheringinn.com/what-we-offer) is a nonprofit based in Rocklin that runs emergency shelter, permanent supportive housing and medical respite care in Placer County. Its South Placer Emergency Shelter serves people in the Roseville area.

If you need a bed tonight, the first step is a phone call to 211. We'll walk through why below.

## Shelter Options Across Placer County

The Gathering Inn runs several shelters. The ones in Placer County are:

- **South Placer Emergency Shelter, Roseville:** Serves individuals and families with children. Church partnerships provide meals and beds, and a medical and dental clinic is on site weekly.
- **Mid Placer Emergency Shelter, Auburn:** Serves adults 18 and older, with meals prepared by community partners.
- **Mobile Temporary Shelter, Auburn:** A low-barrier safe camping model for adults 18 and older, with a strong behavioral health focus.

The Gathering Inn also operates shelters in Sacramento and Elk Grove. Those run under separate arrangements.

## More Than a Bed

Shelter is only where the work starts, since the goal is to help people move from homelessness into stable housing. The Gathering Inn also offers:

- **Permanent supportive housing:** Seven homes across Placer County for the most vulnerable, with case management and life skills education.
- **Housing navigation:** Staff help guests find permanent housing through landlords, housing authorities and a Ready to Rent program.
- **Medical respite:** Short-term care for medically fragile guests, mostly people leaving the hospital. It includes follow-up appointments, medication management and case management.
- **Other support:** Saturday medical triage at the South Placer campus, mental health care, case management and basic needs items.

## How to Get Into Shelter in Roseville, Rocklin or Auburn

The Gathering Inn is part of a coordinated entry system. That means you can't just walk up and ask for a bed. You start with 211.

1. Call 211. It's free and open 24 hours a day. You'll get a referral.
2. Contact the shelter that fits you. The Roseville line, [916-791-5011](tel:+19167915011), accepts adults and families with minor children. The Auburn line, [530-885-8108](tel:+15308858108), accepts adults. The website's Auburn listing has varied, so confirm before you call.
3. If a bed is open, go to your assigned shelter to keep it. If not, you'll be put on a wait list and called when one opens.

## Who Qualifies

Overnight shelter has requirements. The Gathering Inn's site lists proof of Placer County residency, which can include benefits records, an ID with a Placer County street address or a recent utility bill. It also lists probation or parole assignment if it applies, and notes that guests can't be registered sex offenders and must be able to pass a drug and alcohol test.

That's a lot of paperwork for someone in crisis, and it's fair to feel overwhelmed by it. If you aren't sure whether you qualify, call and ask, because the staff can tell you exactly which documents they need.

The main office is at 4020 Sierra College Blvd., Suite 100, Rocklin, CA 95677. The office number is [916-791-9355](tel:+19167919355), and the email is info@thegatheringinn.com.
