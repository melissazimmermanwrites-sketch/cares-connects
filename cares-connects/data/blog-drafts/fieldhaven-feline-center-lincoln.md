[FieldHaven Feline Center](https://fieldhaven.com/) is a cat rescue in Lincoln, California. It finds homes for cats and kittens. It also helps people who care for stray and community cats across Placer County and the Sacramento region.

FieldHaven is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It says it receives no government funding and depends on donations. A thrift store, FieldHaven Marketplace, helps support the work.

## Cat Rescue in Lincoln, Placer County

The center's goal is a solution for every cat in need. That covers a lot of ground, from a single kitten found in a garage to a colony of outdoor cats that needs someone to step in before the population grows.

If you want to adopt a cat or kitten, start by browsing the adoptable cats listed on the website, then reach out to the center. Some adoption hours listed online disagree, and some cats are seen by appointment. Call [916-434-6022](tel:+19164346022) before you drive out.

## Programs That Help Cats and the People Who Care for Them

FieldHaven lists many programs. The core ones are:

- **Trap, Neuter, Return (TNR):** Community cats are humanely trapped, fixed, vaccinated and returned to where they live. The website also has a Sponsor a Colony option.
- **Spay/Neuter Assistance Program (SNAP):** Help getting a pet or community cat fixed.
- **Foster and Kitten Sitters:** Volunteers care for young kittens until they're ready for adoption.
- **Vaccine clinics and medical programs:** Including FIP Stars and a critical care fund.
- **Community and owner assistance:** Programs for neighbors who need help keeping their cats.

The website doesn't post fees for these programs, so it's worth asking about cost and availability when you call.

## FieldHaven Marketplace

The thrift store is at 454 F Street in Lincoln. It sells thrift, vintage, clothing, furniture and upcycled goods, and it's open daily from 10 am to 5 pm. The store phone is [916-434-0622](tel:+19164340622). Every purchase and every donated item supports the cats.

## How to Get Started

1. If you've found a stray, or you're feeding a group of outdoor cats, call FieldHaven at [916-434-6022](tel:+19164346022) and ask about TNR or SNAP.
2. If you can foster, ask about the foster and kitten sitter programs.
3. If you want to adopt, check the cats on the website and call first to confirm hours.

The site also notes that adoption fees are waived on November 14, when cats and kittens go home free. Check the website for details.

A cat in a good home may seem like a small thing, but it changes a household, and fewer unplanned litters change a whole neighborhood. FieldHaven helps make both of those things happen, one cat at a time.
