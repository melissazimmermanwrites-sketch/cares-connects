[La Familia Counseling Center](https://lafcc.org/) has served Sacramento for more than 40 years. It offers multicultural counseling, outreach, job training and family support, with a focus on at-risk youth and families. For many Latino families in Sacramento County, it's a place where you can ask for help in Spanish and be understood.

The mission is to improve the quality of life for at-risk youth and families of diverse backgrounds. Kids, youth, adults and families are all welcome. The website lists services in English, Spanish, Hmong and Vietnamese.

## Where to Find La Familia in Sacramento

The center has two locations in Sacramento:

- **Main office:** 5523 34th Street, Sacramento, CA 95820. Phone: [(916) 452-3601](tel:+19164523601). Open Monday through Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm. It's reachable by Bus Line 61 or the Fruitridge Light Rail Station.
- **Maple Neighborhood Center:** 3301 37th Avenue, Sacramento, CA 95824. Phone: [(916) 210-8773](tel:+19162108773). Open Monday through Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm. After hours emergency line: (916) 227-2600.

You can also email [info@lafcc.org](mailto:info@lafcc.org). Deaf or hard of hearing callers can reach the center through TDD at (800) 735-2929 or 711.

## Programs at the Maple Neighborhood Center

The Maple Neighborhood Center lists several free programs:

- **Behavioral and Physical Health:** Mental health counseling for children, youth and families, ages 0 to 21 with Medi-Cal. Offered in English and Spanish.
- **Centro de Apoyo Latino:** Short-term crisis help and navigation to longer-term support, with an emphasis on Spanish speakers. All ages.
- **Community Health Navigator:** Help with vaccinations, Medi-Cal enrollment, appointments and health workshops. English, Spanish and Dari.
- **Supporting Community Connections:** Suicide prevention resources, training and community support. There's also an older adult program for ages 55 and up.
- **Youth Voice and Youth Pop-Ups:** Leadership workshops for ages 14 to 21, and free community events for ages 12 to 21.

The site also lists partner programs, including a creative writing workshop and the Sacramento Adventure Playground, which is free and needs parent registration.

## Jobs and Education Support

La Familia has an employment and education program for people who want career or education goals. The site also lists volunteer opportunities and the Bill Campos "Hazlo" Scholarship. Call the main office to ask what's open now.

## How to Get Started

The website doesn't describe a set intake process for counseling. Here's the simplest path:

1. Call [(916) 452-3601](tel:+19164523601) or the Maple Neighborhood Center at [(916) 210-8773](tel:+19162108773).
2. Or use the "Talk To Us" contact page on the website, or email the center.
3. Tell them who needs support and which language you prefer.

The counseling for children and youth at Maple is for those with Medi-Cal. If you're not sure you qualify, ask when you call. Program details and schedules can change, so a quick call is worth it.

Asking for help is hard enough, and doing it in your own language makes it a little easier. The staff are there to listen first, and you can bring a family member along to your first call or visit if that helps you feel more comfortable.
