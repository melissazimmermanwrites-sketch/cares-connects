Starting a business means making a hundred decisions before lunch. Funding, taxes, hiring, marketing. You shouldn't have to pay a consultant just to find out where to begin. The [Sacramento Valley Small Business Development Center](https://www.sacramentovalleysbdc.org/), or SBDC, offers business advising at no cost.

The center is at 1792 Tribute Road in Sacramento. It serves Sacramento, Sutter, Yolo and Yuba counties, so if you run a business in any of those, you're in its service area. It's hosted by California Capital FDC, a Sacramento nonprofit that has helped small businesses get capital since 1982. The SBDC is part of the Northern California SBDC program, funded in part by the U.S. Small Business Administration and the California Office of the Small Business Advocate.

## What the Sacramento Valley SBDC Offers

The SBDC says its advising is confidential and "100% pro-bono." That means free, and your business details stay private.

Advisors work with owners at every stage:

- Startups that are still taking shape
- Growing businesses
- Owners planning a transition or sale

Help also includes:

- One-on-one advising
- "Get Funded" resources for access to capital
- Online trainings and webinars, listed on the events calendar
- Guidance on topics like human resources, compliance, marketing and accounting
- Specialty advising for import and export, plus industry programs for restaurants and technology

The center reports that it helped more than 2,461 clients last year. Those clients accessed $86 million in capital and created 491 new jobs.

## Who Can Use the SBDC in Sacramento County

Advising is for for-profit business owners and people starting a business. When you sign up, you'll be asked to certify that you're legally eligible to receive services funded by U.S. taxpayer dollars. You don't need a business plan to begin.

Not sure whether you're in the right place? Call and ask. The staff can point you toward the right resource.

## How to Sign Up

1. Go to the [sign-up page](https://www.sacramentovalleysbdc.org/join/) and fill out the form.
2. Request an advisor, and share what you're working on.
3. Meet with your advisor, and watch the events calendar for trainings that fit.

You can also call [916-655-2100](tel:+19166552100) with questions. The office is at 1792 Tribute Road, Suite 455, Sacramento, CA 95815.

## Capital Help Next Door

California Capital also runs a lending center and a Women's Business Center from the same building, in Suite 270. If your next step is a loan, ask the SBDC about it, or contact California Capital at [916-442-1729](tel:+19164421729).

Whether you're sketching a plan on a napkin or deciding how to sell a business you've run for twenty years, the first conversation is the hardest to start, and the SBDC is set up to make it easier. Good advice early on can save a lot of money and worry later. Here, it costs nothing to ask.
