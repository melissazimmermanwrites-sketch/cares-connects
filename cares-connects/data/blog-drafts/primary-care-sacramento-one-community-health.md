[One Community Health](https://onecommunityhealth.com/) is a nonprofit health center in Sacramento, California. It provides medical, dental and behavioral health care to people regardless of their ability to pay. If you don't have insurance, or you're not sure what you can afford, you can still make an appointment and be seen by a provider.

The organization has served the greater Sacramento area since 1989. Its main campus is at 1500 21st Street in Midtown, and it has health centers in Arden-Arcade and Carmichael as well.

## Health Care at One Community Health in Sacramento

The website lists these services:

- Primary medical care
- Dental care
- Behavioral health
- Vision care
- An on-site pharmacy
- HIV care and prevention
- Specialty care
- Perinatal care
- Street medicine
- A walk-in clinic and laboratory

Medicare, Medi-Cal and Covered California plans are accepted, along with others that the site doesn't name. For people without insurance, the clinic offers a sliding fee based on income. If you're looking for coverage, staff say they'll help you find a plan.

## The Walk-In Clinic

Sometimes you're sick and can't wait two weeks for an appointment, or you don't have a regular doctor yet. The walk-in clinic at 1500 21st Street is first come, first served. No appointment is needed. It's for adults and children, Medi-Cal is accepted, and care is available without insurance.

The website says the walk-in clinic can help with common illnesses and infections, testing for flu, strep and COVID-19, minor injuries such as cuts and splinters, and sexual health testing and treatment. It isn't for emergencies, routine physicals, work-related injuries or controlled substance refills. For a life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

The site mentions extended hours but doesn't list them, so call [916-443-3299](tel:+19164433299) before you go.

## How to Make an Appointment

1. Call [916-443-3299](tel:+19164433299), or use the online scheduling on the Get Care page.
2. If you call after hours, say you're a One Community Health patient.
3. Bring your Medi-Cal card or insurance information, if you have it.
4. If you need the sliding fee, bring proof of income, such as a pay stub or last year's taxes.
5. Bring a list of your medications, past providers' contact information, and your pharmacy's name and address.

Going to the doctor can feel heavy when money is tight and your schedule is full. A health center built to see everyone, whatever their background or income, takes some of that weight off, and it can become the place you return to for years.
