A notice on your door, a benefits letter you don't understand, or a landlord who won't fix the heat can be hard enough to face without also having to pay for a lawyer. [Legal Services of Northern California](https://lsnc.net/office/auburn) (LSNC) provides free civil legal help to low-income people, and its Mother Lode Regional Office in Auburn serves Placer County.

## LSNC in Auburn

The Auburn office is at 190 Reamer Street. It also serves Amador, Calaveras, El Dorado, Nevada and Sierra counties. LSNC itself serves 23 Northern California counties. Its stated mission is to empower the poor to identify and defeat the causes and effects of poverty, and it has offered legal services since 1956.

LSNC handles civil matters. It isn't a criminal defense office.

## What LSNC Can Help With in Placer County

The Auburn office lists help in these areas:

- Housing problems, including eviction
- CalWORKs and CalFresh
- Medi-Cal
- General Assistance
- Social Security and SSI
- Unemployment insurance benefits
- State disability insurance

The LSNC website also offers self-help materials, which can be a good first stop. Whether an attorney takes your case depends on eligibility and availability, so call early.

## Who Qualifies

LSNC serves low-income clients. The Auburn page doesn't list a specific income limit, and I couldn't confirm one, so ask when you call. Bring any papers connected to your problem, such as notices, letters or court documents.

## How to Reach the Auburn Office

1. Call [(530) 823-7560](tel:+15308237560) or toll free [(800) 660-6107](tel:+18006606107).
2. Or walk in at 190 Reamer Street, Auburn, during walk-in hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 12:00 pm and 1:15 pm to 4:00 pm. The office is closed to new intakes on Fridays but serves existing clients.
3. General intake by phone runs Monday to Thursday, 9:00 am to 12:00 pm and 1:15 pm to 4:00 pm.
4. Can't call during the day? Leave a message on the evening intake line at [(866) 815-5990](tel:+18668155990). Calls are returned within one to two business days.
5. If you're deaf or hard of hearing, or phone calls are hard for you, you can make first contact by email at auburn-office@lsnc.net.

Hours can change, so it's worth confirming before you make the trip.

You don't need to have everything figured out before you reach out, so bring whatever papers you have and tell them what happened.

### FAQ

**Does LSNC charge for its help?** LSNC says it provides free civil representation to low-income clients, though whether an attorney can take your case depends on eligibility and availability, which is why calling sooner rather than later makes sense.

**What if I'm not sure my problem is a legal one?** Call anyway and explain what happened in plain words, because intake staff can tell you whether LSNC handles it and, if not, point you toward other legal aid, including the LawHelp California website.
