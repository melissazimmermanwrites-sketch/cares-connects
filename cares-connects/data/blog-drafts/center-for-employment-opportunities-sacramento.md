Coming home after incarceration is hard enough. Finding steady work with a record can feel close to impossible. The [Center for Employment Opportunities](https://www.ceoworks.org/locations/sacramento), known as CEO, has an office in Sacramento that exists for exactly this moment. It provides employment services to people who have recently been released from incarceration.

CEO is a national nonprofit with locations in several communities. The Sacramento office serves people in Sacramento County and works only with people who are newly home. That focus is the point. The staff know the questions you're facing, because they hear them every day.

## What CEO Offers in Sacramento, California

CEO's model is built around work first. Rather than sending you off to fill out applications with nothing to show, it puts you on a paid crew and builds from there. According to CEO, participants complete a job readiness orientation, then work on transitional crews for up to four days a week.

Along the way, the program is designed to include:

- Paid transitional work on a crew
- Job coaching to prepare for a permanent position
- Help finding a full-time job
- Support after you're placed, so you can keep the job

Crews do maintenance and neighborhood beautification work. A day on a crew is a day of work history, a reference and a paycheck.

## Who Can Join CEO Sacramento

CEO serves people who have recently been released from incarceration. If you're on probation or parole, your agent or case manager can refer you. You don't need to have a job lined up. You do need to be recently home.

CEO's Sacramento page lists a referral email for people who want to become participants. A case manager, parole agent or probation officer can also reach out for you. If you'd rather start on your own, send a short email or call the office and ask what the next step is.

## How to Get Started

1. Email [referralssacramento@ceoworks.org](mailto:referralssacramento@ceoworks.org), or ask your parole agent or case manager to send a referral.
2. Call the office at [916-794-4526](tel:+19167944526) with questions.
3. Visit the office at 444 North 3rd Street, Suite 230, Sacramento, CA 95811. Office hours are 8:30 am to 4:30 pm.

Not sure what to bring on your first visit? Call the office and ask, so you walk in prepared and don't have to make a second trip across town.

## A Fair Start

The program is also built around the idea that stable work is one of the strongest foundations for a steady life after release, and that people do better when someone walks the first stretch beside them. A first paycheck after release does more than cover bills. It tells you that the work you do counts, and that you're building something. CEO's Sacramento team is there to help with that first step and the ones that follow.
