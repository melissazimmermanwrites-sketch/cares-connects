**If you or someone you love is in crisis, call or text 988. In Placer County, the adult crisis line is (916) 787-8860 or (888) 886-5401. For immediate danger, call 911.**

[KidsFirst Counseling and Family Resource Centers](https://www.kidsfirstnow.org/) offers counseling, classes and support to kids, young adults, parents and families across Placer County. It has two offices, one in Roseville and one in Auburn. The website says services are available at no cost, in English and Spanish.

Parenting is hard. Some seasons are harder than others. KidsFirst is a place to get help before things feel out of reach.

## Counseling at KidsFirst in Roseville

The counseling page lists these services:

- **Individual, child and family therapy:** For all ages, from young children to seniors.
- **Parent Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT):** For families with children ages 2 to 7 who have behavioral challenges.
- **Trauma Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT):** For children, young adults, parents and families affected by a traumatic event.
- **Perinatal mental health therapy:** For moms during pregnancy and up to 18 months after birth.

Counseling is offered at the two family resource centers. Spanish-language information is on the site under "Servicios en Español."

## Classes and Support Beyond Therapy

The website also lists parenting classes, health and wellness programs, financial literacy classes and support groups. The centers focus on parental resilience, social connections, concrete support in times of need, knowledge of parenting and child development, and children's social and emotional skills.

Because the same organization runs both the counseling and the family resource centers, you can often connect with more than one kind of help in a single place. A parent who comes in for a class, for example, can also ask about therapy for a child, and a mom expecting a baby can ask about perinatal support.

**Auburn:** 200 Auburn Folsom Rd., Suite 101, Auburn, CA 95603. Phone: [(530) 887-3536](tel:+15308873536).

Both offices are open Monday through Thursday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm, and Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. They close for some holidays, so call ahead.

## How to Request Services

You don't need to figure out which therapy fits. Here's how to start:

1. Go to the Request Services page at [kidsfirstnow.org/services](https://www.kidsfirstnow.org/services/).
2. Fill out the referral form, in English or Spanish.
3. Or call the Roseville or Auburn office.

You can ask for help for yourself, your child or your family. The website doesn't say how long it takes to get an appointment, so ask when you call.

## A Note on Limits

Therapy takes time. It isn't emergency help. If you're worried about someone's safety right now, use the crisis numbers at the top of this post.

Asking for support isn't a sign you're failing. It usually means you care enough to look for more tools, and the staff at the Roseville office on Gibson Drive can help you find the right starting point, whether that's therapy, a class or a support group for your family.
