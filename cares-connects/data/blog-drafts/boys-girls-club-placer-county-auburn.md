The [Boys & Girls Club of Placer County](https://bgcplacercounty.org/) gives kids in Auburn and Roseville a safe, structured place to go after school. It also runs programs during holiday breaks and summer.

The Club opened its doors in 1994, and its website says it serves hundreds of young people each day, which makes it a steady part of family life in the foothills. Its main site is the Mullin & McAdams Clubhouse & Teen Center at 679 Lincoln Way in Auburn.

## What Kids Do at the Club in Auburn, Placer County

The Club's programs cover:

- Education
- Character development
- Career exploration
- Sports and recreation
- The arts
- Community service

For working parents, that means a place where your child is supervised and busy until 6:00 pm, with homework help and activities built into the afternoon. For kids, it's a place to belong, to try something new and to spend time with caring adults.

## Where the Club Meets

The Club operates from four sites in Auburn and Roseville, and the grade levels served differ by location:

- **Mullin & McAdams Clubhouse & Teen Center, 679 Lincoln Way, Auburn:** TK through 5th grade, and 6th through 12th. Phone: [530-889-2273](tel:+15308892273).
- **E.V. Cain Extension, 150 Palm Avenue, Auburn:** TK/K through 8th grade.
- **Auburn Elementary Extension, 11400 Lariat Ranch Rd, Auburn:** TK/K through 8th grade. Phone: [530-718-4110](tel:+15307184110).
- **Union Pacific Railroad Clubhouse, 313 High Street, Roseville:** 1st through 8th grade. Phone: [916-789-7884](tel:+19167897884).

Clubs generally run from after school until 6:00 pm, five days a week. At Mullin & McAdams, the doors open at 2:45 pm Tuesday through Friday and 1:15 pm on Monday.

## What It Costs

Membership is $10 a month per child, although extra fees may apply during school breaks and summer when the Club offers longer days. The website doesn't mention scholarships or financial help, so if cost is a worry, call your site and ask.

## How to Sign Up

1. Visit the registration page on the Club's website and fill out the after-school form.
2. If your child lands on a waitlist, don't pay until the Club contacts you.
3. Pay the monthly fee through the payment portal on the membership page.

Spring break and summer camps are open to kids in 1st through 12th grade, ages 6 to 18, and they require an after-school membership. Registration for the 2026-2027 after-school year and Thanksgiving Break Camp is open now, and Winter Break Camp registration is listed as coming soon.

Parents of TK and kindergarten kids at Mullin & McAdams need to arrange transportation.

## Supporting the Club

The Club says it costs $100 a month to give one child everything it offers. Donations help close that gap.
