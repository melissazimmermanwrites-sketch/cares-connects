[Powerhouse Ministries](https://www.phmfolsom.org/) is a Christian nonprofit in Folsom, California, that offers no-cost residential recovery programs and a walk-in Help Center. It serves people who are trying to leave behind homelessness, addiction, abuse, sex trafficking, mental health struggles or incarceration.

The website says Powerhouse has spent more than 30 years working with people in Folsom and the surrounding area. It describes its mission as helping people rise above their circumstances, change their lives and make a difference in their community. The programs are faith-based, and the site doesn't spell out the rules for how faith fits into daily life, so ask when you call.

## Powerhouse Ministries Housing in Folsom, Sacramento County

Powerhouse runs residential recovery programs that are described as no-cost:

- **Women and Children's Center:** For women, with or without their children.
- **Men's Recovery Center:** For men.
- **Leadership College:** A no-cost residential program for young men and women ages 18 to 24, with life coaching, hands-on internships, mentorship and career placement.

You're not signing up for a quick fix here. These are residential programs, and the work takes time. Ask staff how long a stay usually lasts and what the day-to-day schedule looks like, because the website doesn't say.

## The Help Center in Folsom

If you aren't ready for a residential program, or you just need a place to start, the Help Center is the front door. The site describes it as a place for people who need immediate resources, along with a long-term plan.

When you visit, a team member sits down with you to talk through your immediate needs and the bigger picture. According to the website, staff can help with:

- Homeless prevention and connections to temporary and permanent housing
- Referrals to treatment centers
- Job readiness training
- Help with government benefits and recovery programs
- One-on-one pastoral care, prayer, case management and counseling

The Help Center also offers showers, a medical clinic and personal cell phones on a monthly rotation. These are free. Ask the office which week they're offered.

## How to Get Help from Powerhouse Ministries

1. Visit the office at 11 Natoma Street, Folsom, CA 95630, during open hours, or call [916-983-0658](tel:+19169830658).
2. Meet with a Help Center team member to talk about what you need right now.
3. Ask about the residential programs if you want to apply. The website has a Get Help page, and staff can walk you through it.

Office hours are Sunday, 3:00 to 5:00 pm. Monday through Wednesday, 9:00 am to 4:00 pm. Thursday, 9:00 am to 12:00 pm. The office is closed Friday and Saturday.

If you're helping someone else, such as a son, a sister or a friend, you can call too. Powerhouse can tell you what the next step looks like.

Getting started is often the hardest part. This is a place where someone will meet you where you are and help you plan what comes next.
