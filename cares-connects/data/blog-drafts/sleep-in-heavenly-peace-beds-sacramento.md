Every child deserves a bed. [Sleep in Heavenly Peace (SHP), CA-Sacramento Chapter](https://shpbeds.org/chapter/ca-sacramento/) builds and delivers free twin beds to kids in Sacramento County who are sleeping on the floor. The chapter is run by volunteers.

SHP is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit with chapters across the country. The Sacramento chapter's president is Amber Jewison, and its volunteers build the beds by hand.

SHP's mission is simple: no kid should sleep on the floor. Beds go to children who lack one of their own, and the application page says SHP makes and delivers twin size beds as supplies and donations allow.

## Who Can Get a Bed in Sacramento

SHP's application page sets out the basics:

- The child must be 3 to 17 years old.
- You must be the child's legal guardian, or have a referral from a school, social service agency, family member or other local agency.
- You must live in a zip code covered by an active SHP chapter that is taking applications.
- Your home must be accessible and have a room big enough for a twin bed.
- SHP must be able to reach you by phone, text or email.

If you're accepted, you'll sign an indemnification release form before delivery.

## How to Apply for a Bed

Getting started is simple:

1. Go to the online "Apply for a Bed" form at [shpbeds.org/apply](https://shpbeds.org/apply/).
2. Check your zip code in the first section. It tells you whether a chapter covers your area.
3. Complete the SHP application form.
4. Watch your email for a confirmation. Read it carefully.

If you've applied before, you can update your contact information with the link on the apply page. You'll need the application ID from your confirmation email.

There's no office to visit, because this is a volunteer chapter. For questions, call [844-432-2337, extension 5139](tel:+18444322337,5139) or use the contact form at [shpbeds.org/contact-us](https://shpbeds.org/contact-us/).

## What to Expect

SHP makes and delivers twin beds as supplies and donations allow. Selection depends on availability and need. Applications that aren't picked right away are filed until more beds can be made, and SHP can't promise every applicant a bed. The website doesn't give a wait time.

Families in Placer County should check their zip code on the apply form first.

The chapter's volunteer coordinator and its other team members manage build days and donations, so if you're thinking of volunteering, you can reach out through the contact page and ask where help is needed most.

## How to Help in Sacramento

You can help put a kid in a bed. The chapter page lists ways to give:

- **Volunteer:** find build days at volunteer.shpbeds.org.
- **Donate bedding:** new twin sheets, pillows and bedding can be sent from the chapter's Walmart wishlist.
- **Donate money:** use the Donate link at shpbeds.org.
- **Sponsor a build:** contact the chapter through its contact page.

A bed is a small thing that changes a lot. A child with a bed of their own sleeps better, and a tired parent can rest a little easier too.
