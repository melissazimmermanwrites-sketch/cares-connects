[CASA of Placer, Yuba and Sutter](https://casaconnects.org/), also called CASA Connects, trains community volunteers to stand beside children in foster care. Its headquarters is in Roseville, in Placer County. It also runs Be Bold, a mentorship program for older kids and teens.

If you've ever wondered how one adult can change a young person's direction, this is a good place to look, because some kids simply need a steady person who shows up week after week, and CASA trains and supports the people who do.

## What CASA Does in Roseville and Placer County

CASA stands for Court Appointed Special Advocate. These are trained volunteers who get to know a child in foster care, build trust over time, and speak up for that child's needs in court, at school and in the community.

The organization serves Placer, Yuba and Sutter counties. Its Roseville office is the headquarters for Placer County, and a second office serves Yuba and Sutter counties from Yuba City.

## Be Bold Mentorship

Be Bold is a mentoring program for youth ages 10 to 21. It's for young people who:

- Are at risk of, or are experiencing, commercial sexual exploitation
- Are at risk of, or involved in, the juvenile justice system
- Are involved in juvenile dependency or delinquency court
- Are referred voluntarily from the community

Mentors are trained CASAs. Each mentor and youth meet weekly. Every other month, mentees and mentors also join a group session.

## How to Refer a Young Person to Be Bold

You don't have to be a caseworker to make a referral. The website points families and community members to an online form.

1. Go to the [Be Bold referral form](https://casaconnects.org/be-bold/referral/) and fill it out.
2. Or call [(530) 887-1006](tel:+15308871006) with questions about eligibility.

## How to Become a CASA Volunteer

CASA volunteers come from everyday life. You don't need a social work background. Here's what the website asks of you:

- Be over 21
- Have a California driver's license with a three-year safe driving history
- Communicate well, in speaking and in writing
- Pass criminal and CPS history background checks
- Commit to at least two years, about 8 to 10 hours a month, for one child

Training is 32 hours at the start, then 12 hours of continuing education each year. The steps are simple: attend an info session, submit an application, interview, finish training and background checks, get sworn in, and be matched with a child. You can start with the [info session form](https://casaconnects.org/info-session-form/).
