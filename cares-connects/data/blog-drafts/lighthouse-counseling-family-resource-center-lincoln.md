[Lighthouse Counseling & Family Resource Center](https://lighthousefrc.org/) is based in Lincoln, in Placer County. It offers no-to-low cost counseling, classes and help with everyday needs. The website says it serves more than 5,000 Placer County residents a year and has operated for over 20 years.

If you're in crisis, call or text 988, the national Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or call 911 in an emergency. Lighthouse is for ongoing support.

## What Lighthouse Does in Lincoln and Placer County

Lighthouse helps individuals and families of all ages heal and build safety and stability. Its counseling covers anxiety, depression, grief, trauma and suicidal thoughts. Sessions are available in person and by telehealth, in English and Spanish.

## Counseling and Family Support at Lighthouse

The website lists these services:

- Individual, couples, family and adolescent counseling
- Group counseling
- Intensive case management
- Postpartum counseling and a New Mom Home Visitation Program
- Help enrolling in Medi-Cal and CalFresh
- A Diaper Bank
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

## Classes at Lighthouse

The site lists educational classes on anger management, parenting, grief, self-care and stress reduction. Examples include Good Grief, DBT: Understanding & Managing Emotions, Self Care and Love & Logic Parenting. There's also a Spanish-language women's group. The homepage also mentions car seat safety and holiday assistance. Class schedules change, so ask what's coming up.

Lighthouse also names reducing child abuse, domestic violence, anxiety and depression as part of its mission. That means the staff understand how stress at home affects everyone living there, from toddlers to grandparents. Whether you're struggling after a loss or adjusting to life with a new baby, you can ask about the option that matches your situation.

## Where Lighthouse Is

Lighthouse lists offices in Lincoln, Roseville, Auburn and Rocklin. The only address on the website is the Lincoln mailing address:

110 Gateway Drive, Suite 210, Lincoln, CA 95648

Phone: [(916) 645-3300](tel:+19166453300)

Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:00 am to 4:30 pm. The office closes for lunch from 12:00 to 1:00 pm.

Since that's listed as a mailing address, call before you visit.

## How to Request Services

The website doesn't list eligibility rules or exact fees. It describes services as no-to-low cost. Here's how to start:

1. Fill out the online referral form on the website.
2. Or use the Contact page.
3. Or call [(916) 645-3300](tel:+19166453300) during office hours.

The site doesn't describe the intake steps, so expect a staff member to follow up and explain what comes next. You can ask about cost in that first call.

## Why a Family Resource Center Matters

Hard times rarely come one at a time. A family may need counseling, help signing up for food assistance and diapers all at once. Lighthouse puts those under one roof, so you're not starting from scratch at three different offices, and one staff member can help you figure out which service fits first. If you live in Roseville, Auburn or Rocklin, the website lists offices there too, so ask which location is closest when you call.
