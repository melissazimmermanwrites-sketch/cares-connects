Getting older brings practical questions about who can bring a hot meal, how to get to the doctor, and where to even start. [Seniors First, Inc.](https://seniorsfirst.org/) is a nonprofit based at 2543 Warren Drive in Rocklin, and it exists to answer those questions for older adults across Placer County.

The organization says it has been making a difference for more than 50 years and delivers over 125,000 essential services each year, and most of those services begin with a single phone call.

## Seniors First in Rocklin: Where to Start

The Information and Assistance line is the front door. You can call it yourself, or a daughter, a neighbor or a caregiver can make the call on your behalf.

Staff help Placer County residents age 60 and older find resources to stay safe, healthy and independent. Topics include adult day care, care options, Medicare counseling, transportation and veterans services. If you live outside Placer County or don't meet the age requirement, they may still be able to point you to another agency.

## How Seniors First Supports Older Adults in Placer County

Programs listed on the Seniors First website include:

- **Meals on Wheels:** Home-delivered meals for Placer County seniors who are homebound and unable to shop or cook. Meals come three times a week. The suggested contribution is $6.00 per meal, and no one is turned away for not paying.
- **Senior Cafés:** Hot lunches at cafés in Auburn, Rocklin and Roseville on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 11:00 am to 1:00 pm. Reservations are required.
- **Lincoln Senior Center:** Weekday lunches and activities.
- **AniMEALS:** Fresh pet food delivered along with Meals on Wheels.
- **Transportation:** Rides for seniors. The organization reports 10,248 rides last year, covering more than 90,000 miles.
- **Telephone Reassurance:** Regular calls to seniors who live alone. Volunteers and staff gave over 1,000 hours of calls last year.
- **Living Placement:** Help finding an assisted living community that fits.

Last year Seniors First served 143,180 hot meals and gave out 24,961 pounds of groceries.

Meals on Wheels covers the western slope of Placer County, but not the Tahoe-Truckee area. Eligibility depends on age, residence and need, so it's best to ask when you call.

## How to Get Help From Seniors First

1. Call [(800) 878-9222](tel:+18008789222) or [(530) 889-9500](tel:+15308899500).
2. Tell the person who answers what's going on and what you need.
3. They'll help you find the right program, or the right agency if it isn't theirs.
4. For Meals on Wheels or a café lunch, call (530) 889-9500 to sign up or reserve a seat.

You can also email info@seniorsfirst.org. The office is at 2543 Warren Drive, Rocklin, CA 95677.

You don't have to figure it out alone, because a hot lunch, a ride to an appointment or a friendly voice on the phone can help someone stay independent for a long time.

### FAQ

**Do I have to be a Placer County resident?** Seniors First serves Placer County residents age 60 and older, and family members and caregivers are welcome to call on a senior's behalf, even if they live somewhere else.

**What if I can't afford the suggested contribution for meals?** The website says seniors who can't pay will not be denied meals, so please don't let cost keep you from calling and asking about Meals on Wheels or a Senior Café lunch near you.
