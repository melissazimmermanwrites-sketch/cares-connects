A flat tire can end a work commute. A broken chain can leave you stranded. The [Sacramento Bicycle Kitchen](https://www.sacbikekitchen.org/) is a volunteer-run, do-it-yourself bike shop in Midtown Sacramento, and it's built so you can fix the problem yourself.

The shop is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It's run by volunteers and relies on donations for its revenue. It's near 20th and I Streets, at 1915 I Street, Sacramento. Look for the ivy.

## How the Sacramento Bike Kitchen Works

You bring your bike. The shop provides the space, the tools and help from staff and volunteers. You do the repair, and you learn how it's done. The shop asks for a minimal cash daily membership fee.

The idea is simple: knowledge and tools should be easy to share. That approach keeps cycling affordable for Sacramento County neighbors who rely on a bike to get to work, school or the store. It also builds skills you can use the next time something breaks.

The shop also offers:

- Used bikes and parts for sale
- Maintenance and safety classes
- Monthly public shifts for women, trans, femme, genderqueer and nonbinary people
- Programs for kids and families

Ask at the shop about current class schedules and what's available.

## Hours at the Bike Kitchen

Regular hours are:

- Tuesday: 6 to 9 pm
- Wednesday: 7 to 9 pm
- Thursday: 6 to 9 pm
- Friday: 10 am to 2 pm
- Sunday: 10 am to 1 pm

Volunteers staff every shift, so hours can be canceled when there aren't enough people. Check the calendar on the website before you go. The shop phone, [916-538-6697](tel:+19165386697), is mostly answered during open hours. You can also email info@sacbikekitchen.org.

## How to Donate Bikes and Parts

Drop donations off during open shop hours. Clerks can give you a receipt on request. The shop takes:

- Usable bike parts, even dirty or incomplete ones
- Higher-quality bikes, such as Trek, Specialized, Fuji and Marin, if usable parts remain
- Bike tools and accessories

It can't take low-quality department store bikes in poor condition, or rusted and broken items with no usable parts. The shop manager on duty decides what to accept. For large donations, email donations@sacbikekitchen.org.

## How to Volunteer

The shop says volunteer time is the most valuable thing you can give. Visit the Become a Volunteer page on the website, or write to volunteer@sacbikekitchen.org. You don't have to be an expert. Many people start by showing up and asking questions.

You can also give a cash or PayPal donation, which is tax deductible.

Because the shop runs on volunteers and donations, every bike, part and hour of help keeps the doors open for the next person who needs a ride. When you give a bike a second life, you give someone a way to get around.
