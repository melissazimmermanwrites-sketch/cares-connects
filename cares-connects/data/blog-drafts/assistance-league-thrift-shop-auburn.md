If you've ever found the perfect jacket for a few dollars, you know the small joy of a good thrift shop. The [Assistance League of Greater Placer Thrift Shop](https://chapters.assistanceleague.org/chapter/greaterplacer/thrift-shop) in Auburn, California, adds something more. Your purchase helps pay for community programs in Placer County.

The shop is run by Assistance League of Greater Placer, a nonprofit chapter of volunteers who work to improve lives for children and adults in the area. The shop is open to everyone, with no sign-up and no proof of need.

## What You'll Find at the Auburn Thrift Shop

The shop sells low-cost items for the whole household. Expect to find:

- Clothing for adults and children
- Baby clothes and accessories
- Housewares and linens
- Home decor
- Sporting goods
- Jewelry and antiques

Stock changes as donations come in, so it's worth stopping by more than once. Come with an open mind and a little time to browse, and you may leave with something you didn't know you needed.

## Shop Hours and Location

The shop is open Tuesday through Saturday, 11 am to 4 pm, and it closes on holidays, including Thanksgiving, December 25 and January 1. It's on Grass Valley Highway in Auburn, Placer County. Call [530-885-1982](tel:+15308851982) for directions or to ask about an item.

## How to Donate Clothing and Household Goods

The shop depends on gently used donations. Drop-offs are accepted Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday, 11 am to 3 pm. The donation area is in the back left corner of the same shopping center as the shop.

A few tips:

1. Bring clean, gently used clothing and household items during donation hours.
2. For furniture and other large items, call first. The shop prefers these on Wednesdays, and management must approve them.
3. Check with the shop about what it can accept. Over time it has stopped taking some items, so ask before you load the car.

Donation details have changed over the years, and the chapter's pages don't always match, so a quick phone call saves you a trip.

## Where the Money Goes

Shop sales support the chapter's philanthropic work in the Greater Placer area. The chapter's focus is helping children and adults through volunteer-run programs. Because the shop is staffed by volunteers, what you spend goes toward that mission.

Because the shop is open to everyone, there's no paperwork and no need to explain yourself. You walk in, look around, and pay a low price for what you find. If you're furnishing a first apartment or refreshing a closet on a tight budget, that kind of easy access matters. Whether you need a winter coat, a set of dishes or a place to send what you've outgrown, this shop is a practical choice close to home.
