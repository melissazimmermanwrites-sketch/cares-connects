A job interview is stressful enough, and not having the right clothes can make it feel impossible. [Dress for Success Sacramento](https://sacramento.dressforsuccess.org/), based in North Sacramento, California, is part of an international network that helps women get back on their feet by providing professional attire, a support network and career mentoring.

## About Dress for Success Sacramento

The Sacramento group is a local affiliate of Dress for Success, a worldwide nonprofit. It was registered as a California nonprofit in 2014, according to public business records. Its office is at 4215 Norwood Avenue, Suite 3B, Sacramento, CA 95838.

Its mission is to help women reach economic independence. That means more than a suit. It means having someone in your corner while you look for work, and after you land the job, when the first weeks can be the hardest.

## How Dress for Success Helps Women in Sacramento County

Dress for Success affiliates generally offer a mix of:

- Professional clothing for interviews
- Career mentoring
- Networking and support groups
- Help staying on the job once you're hired

Each affiliate sets its own programs and rules. The Sacramento group's website was not available when this post was written, so please confirm the exact services, hours and how to qualify by phone before you make the trip.

## How to Get Started

1. Call [(916) 243-1282](tel:+19162431282) or email sacramento@dressforsuccess.org.
2. Ask how to request clothing and whether you need a referral from a partner agency. Some affiliates work that way.
3. Ask about mentoring, groups and other support, and whether you need an appointment.

If you work with a job center, shelter or county office, ask your caseworker if they already partner with Dress for Success.

## Before You Call

It helps to have a few details ready. Know your interview date, if you have one. Know your clothing sizes, and the best number to reach you. If you're working with a job center, shelter or county office, bring the name of your caseworker, since they may be the one who makes the connection.

You deserve to walk into that interview feeling like yourself, and ready.
