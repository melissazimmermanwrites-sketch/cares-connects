You're doing a lot. You help with meals, pills, appointments and bad nights. You may not have had a break in months. [Del Oro Caregiver Resource Center](https://www.deloro.org/services), based in Citrus Heights, supports unpaid family caregivers in Sacramento County, Placer County and 11 other counties.

Del Oro was founded in 1987. It's a private nonprofit and one of 11 regional Caregiver Resource Centers in California, funded in part by the California Department of Aging. Its website says all services come at no cost to the family caregiver.

## Who Del Oro Helps

Del Oro serves the caregiver, not only the person receiving care. You qualify if you're caring for either of these:

- An adult with a cognitive impairing condition that began after age 18, such as dementia, Alzheimer's, stroke, traumatic brain injury or Parkinson's
- Someone 60 or older, with any diagnosis, who needs help with two or more activities of daily living

The 13 counties served are Alpine, Amador, Calaveras, Colusa, El Dorado, Nevada, Placer, Sacramento, San Joaquin, Sierra, Sutter, Yolo and Yuba.

## Services for Caregivers in Sacramento and Placer Counties

Based on your situation, support may include:

- **Family consultation and case management:** a needs assessment and care planning
- **Respite care:** time off, at home or somewhere else, through contracted agencies
- **Support groups**
- **Short-term counseling:** up to six one-hour individual sessions
- **Legal and financial consultation** with an attorney
- **Education and training**
- **GUIDE by Tembo:** a dementia support program at no cost to people with Traditional Medicare

The website also lists specialized information on conditions, aging and community resources, which can help when a new diagnosis leaves you with more questions than answers. Respite matters because caregiving is physical and emotional work. Rest isn't a luxury. It's how you keep going.

Del Oro's services page describes help for unpaid family caregivers of adults with chronic or disabling conditions, so the focus is on the person doing the daily work at home, whatever the diagnosis turns out to be.

## How to Reach Del Oro

The website doesn't spell out an intake process, so the best first step is a call.

1. Call [916-728-9333](tel:+19167289333).
2. Or use the Contact Us page on the website.
3. Tell them who you care for and what's hardest right now.

Del Oro's office is at 8421 Auburn Blvd., Suite 265, Citrus Heights, CA 95610.

You don't need to have every answer before you call. A family consultation starts with your situation, and the care plan grows from there. Many caregivers wait until they're exhausted to ask for help, and calling sooner is allowed.

## Good to Know

Respite is arranged through contracted agencies, so ask what's available when you call. The services page doesn't list office hours, so call first rather than drop in.

Asking for help doesn't mean you're failing. It means you're planning to keep going.
