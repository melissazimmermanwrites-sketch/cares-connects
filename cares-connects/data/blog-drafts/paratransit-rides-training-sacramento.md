Getting to a doctor's appointment or the grocery store shouldn't depend on whether you have a car or can drive one. [Paratransit, Inc.](https://paratransit.org/) is a nonprofit mobility agency in Sacramento, California. It helps people get around Sacramento County through travel training, community shuttles and support for other agencies.

The office is at 2501 Florin Road, Sacramento, CA 95822. The main phone number is [(916) 429-2009](tel:+19164292009).

## Mobility Training with Paratransit, Inc.

Mobility training teaches you to ride buses and light rail on your own. It is free for qualified individuals. A trainer works with you one-on-one or in a group, on a schedule that fits your needs.

Training can cover:

- Getting to and from the bus stop or light rail station
- Planning a route
- Learning the way to a specific destination, including landmarks
- Using a mobility aid on public transit

Service areas include Sacramento, Carmichael, Fair Oaks, Rancho Cordova, Citrus Heights, Rio Linda, Orangevale, North Highlands, West Sacramento, Davis, Woodland, Roseville, Rocklin, Elk Grove and Folsom.

To ask about training, call (916) 429-2009, extension 7232, or email AGarcia@paratransit.org. The website doesn't list eligibility rules for "qualified individuals," so ask when you call.

## Home to Healthcare Shuttle in Sacramento County

Home to Healthcare gets people to medical care. It is free and open to the general public. There is no preference based on age, disability, income or trip purpose.

- It runs throughout Sacramento County, Monday through Friday, between 10 a.m. and 1 p.m. It does not run on holidays.
- Riders with wheelchairs or mobility devices can board every bus at every stop.
- Door-to-door pickups need a reservation. Request one no earlier than two days before your trip and no later than 5 p.m. the day before.
- Reservations are taken Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., at (916) 429-2009, or 711 for TTY.

## Other Ways Paratransit, Inc. Helps

The organization also runs People to Produce, which takes riders from their neighborhoods to local farmers' markets, so fresh food is easier to reach. Check the website or call for the current schedule. Paratransit, Inc. also provides contract transportation for other human service agencies and maintains vehicles for nonprofit fleets.

Paratransit, Inc. is separate from SacRT, which runs the region's ADA paratransit service. If you need that service, contact SacRT.

The goal is independence. A ride, or the confidence to take the bus yourself, can open up the whole week, and one phone call is enough to find out which option fits you.
