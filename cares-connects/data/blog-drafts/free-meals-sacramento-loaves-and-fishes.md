[Loaves & Fishes](https://www.sacloaves.org/) is a nonprofit campus at 1351 North C Street in Sacramento, California, that feeds and serves people experiencing homelessness. Its dining room serves a free midday meal 364 days a year. Around the meal, you'll find a day space for adults, showers, a free school for children, counseling and a legal clinic, all on one campus near downtown.

The organization began in 1983, when Chris and Dan Delany served sandwiches from the trunks of their cars. Today the website says the organization operates 16 programs and services on a 4.5-acre campus. It does not accept government funds, and it says its work is carried by the generosity of individual supporters, local businesses and community foundations.

## The Dining Room in Sacramento

The dining room serves a midday meal to up to 800 adults daily, every day of the year except Thanksgiving. The website lists these hours:

- Monday through Friday: 11:30 am to 1:00 pm
- Saturday and Sunday: 11:00 am to 12:30 pm

For questions about meals, call [916-637-2450](tel:+19166372450). The website doesn't describe any sign-up or eligibility rules for the dining room, so if you're unsure about anything, a quick phone call will answer it.

## Friendship Park, Maryhouse and Sister Nora's Place

**Friendship Park** is the campus's day space for adult guests. The website lists daily breakfast and coffee, restrooms, hot showers, survival supplies such as sleeping bags and tents, a place to rest, and referrals. A men's Washhouse offers showers, laundry and a clothing exchange. Call [916-443-7378](tel:+19164437378).

**Maryhouse** is a daytime drop-in center for women and families experiencing homelessness. It's open Monday through Friday, 7:00 am to 2:00 pm, although it closes earlier, at 1:00 pm, on Wednesdays. Breakfast is 8:00 to 8:45 am for single women, families, and single fathers with children present. Showers run from 7:00 am to 1:30 pm, first come, first served. Maryhouse is not a shelter, although staff can share information about other resources and help you get on waitlists for housing. Call [916-446-4961](tel:+19164464961).

**Sister Nora's Place** is a long-term overnight shelter with case management for up to 16 women facing chronic homelessness, trauma and serious health problems. There's no set limit on how long someone can stay. Services include private rooms, health evaluations, support groups and life skills classes. Call [916-669-7000](tel:+19166697000).

## More Help on the Campus

- **Mustard Seed School:** A free school for children ages 3 to 15 who are experiencing homelessness. It runs Monday through Friday, 7:30 am to 3:30 pm. Call [916-447-3626](tel:+19164473626).
- **Genesis Mental Health:** Free counseling, support groups and on-campus crisis help, Monday through Friday. Call [916-669-1536](tel:+19166691536).
- **Loaves & Fishes Clinic:** Drop-in primary and urgent care, open five days a week. Ask for hours.
- **Anneke's Haven:** A kennel for guests' pets, open seven days a week. Pets need current shot records and must be spayed or neutered.
- **Legal Clinic:** Call [916-446-0368](tel:+19164460368).

## How to Get Help

1. Come to 1351 North C Street, Sacramento, CA 95811, during meal or program hours.
2. Not sure where to start? Call the main line at [916-446-0874](tel:+19164460874).
3. Ask staff about the other programs and services while you're there. The website says guests are served without judgment or barrier.

You don't have to figure out the whole campus on your first visit, and nobody expects you to. A warm meal is a good place to start, and the people who work and volunteer there can show you the rest when you're ready.
