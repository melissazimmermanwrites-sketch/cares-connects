We care enough to improve the system.

CARES Connects is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our team includes educators, veterans, and consultants who have advised federal, state, and county projects. We're on a mission to improve access to available resources, so we can help strengthen individuals, families, and communities.

Our mission is to close the gap between available support and the people who need it most.
