CARES connects communities

Community Alliance of Resources, Education, and Support 501(c)(3) is a community-centered nonprofit that uses data and technology to close the access gap between individuals and the local programs designed to support them. By building intelligent tools, identifying service gaps, and improving equitable awareness, CARES strengthens community outcomes across multiple need categories.

We are on a mission to help people find their way through life’s most difficult moments.

Our vision

We envision a connected, data-informed community where every person, regardless of background or circumstance, can quickly find and access the support they need to thrive.

CARES Connects strives to become a national model for how technology, collaboration, and local expertise can work together to close service gaps, advance equity, and build stronger, more resilient communities.

Our story

CARES was founded after personal experiences trying to find local supportive resources. In our personal and professional experiences, we kept running into the same challenges: help was out there, but finding it was not easy or intuitive.

At first, our founders considered ways to help individuals one by one. But we soon realized we could have a greater impact on our communities if we helped many at the same time.

When someone needs food, housing, mental health support, transportation, childcare, legal assistance, or another community resource, the hardest part is knowing where to begin.

CARES removes that uncertainty. Using intelligent technology and local partnerships, CARES creates clear pathways between people and the resources that can change their lives.

Our team

Our team combines strategic, technical, and community expertise: educators skilled in human-centered, community-supported approaches, veterans experienced in mission execution, and consultants who have supported data-driven policy and technological development at every level of government.

Together, we are uniquely equipped to bridge the gap between innovation and implementation, and to translate complex systems into simple, accessible tools that deliver measurable community impact.
